1 The price story

Retail price of a 5 kg bag of Koshihikari in Tokyo's 23 wards. Prices sat near ¥2,000–2,500 for over a decade, almost doubled within a year from August 2024, and have been falling since late 2025.

2 Wholesale leads retail

The wholesale price that collectors such as JA receive is repriced each September when the new crop arrives. Retail follows over the next months. The gap between the two, the retailer's mark-up, is the best early signal of where shelf prices go next.

3 What drove the spike

Rice in Japan is almost all grown at home, so the spike came from the domestic harvest and stockpiles rather than world markets. The 2023 summer in Niigata, Japan's top Koshihikari region, was the hottest on record and nearly rainless in August, which hurt milling quality and drew down stocks.
